MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.4 - THE UNDERWORLD
=====================================

(Everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.4: the version is now beta-1.4. Worlds you already have are fine.)


DUCKING
-------
  * Press C to duck (or hold S while you're standing on the ground). You crouch down to 1.4 blocks tall,
    shuffle along slowly, and you can't walk off a ledge (like sneaking in Minecraft).
  * You stand back up when there's room above your head.
  * Friends see you duck too.
  * Cursor Mode has moved from C to LEFT CTRL. It switches when you LET GO of Ctrl, so Ctrl+Q still drops
    a whole stack. (If you had already set your own key for Cursor Mode, it stays.)
  * Both keys can be changed in Options > Controls.


FOOTBALL  (a new add-on, for multiplayer)
-----------------------------------------
  Turn it on in Create New World > Add-ons. Everyone gets a FOOTBALL (craft more: 4 Leather + 1 String).
  * Hold the mouse button to WIND UP. A meter fills over your head: let go in the GREEN for a perfect spiral.
    Hold too long and the meter falls back again, so you have to time it.
  * AIM with the mouse: a dotted line shows the arc the ball will take.
  * CATCH it by clicking just as it reaches you. Right on time: PERFECT CATCH! Too late, and if it's coming
    in fast, you FUMBLE it. A slow ball you just grab. Walk over a ball on the ground to pick it up.
  * Real physics: gravity (Moon Gravity makes it float), air drag, a perfect spiral cuts through the air
    while a bad throw wobbles, it casts a shadow, and a pointy ball bounces crazily and rolls end over end.
  * A football that lands in lava (or falls out of the world) goes back to whoever threw it.
  * New advancement: Touchdown! (a perfect catch). It unlocks the Eye Black accessory.
  * It needs at least two players: you can't throw it alone.


THE UNDERWORLD  (a new dimension)
---------------------------------
  HOW TO GET THERE: craft a HELLBREAKER (2 Ember Ingots with a Block of Diamond between them on top,
  2 Obsidian as the handle). It's a pickaxe that breaks BEDROCK. Dig all the way down, through the
  bedrock at the bottom of the world... and fall. You drift down into the Underworld through a storm of
  embers (that fall never hurts).
  HOW TO GET BACK: dig up through the Underworld's bedrock ceiling (you come out near the bottom of your
  world), or ask the Lost Soul to send you home. If you die down there you wake up where you came in
  (or in a bed you put down there).

  WHAT IT'S LIKE:
    * A gigantic burning cavern under everything: a ceiling of brimstone with stalactites, LAVA SEAS in
      the low ground, great spires of rock, FLOATING ISLANDS of ash, and lava falling out of the ceiling.
    * Behind it all: a cavern that glows from below, jagged spires, far-off ruined towers with burning
      windows, distant lavafalls, and embers drifting up. Everything has a hot red cast; the dark is red-black.
    * Its own MUSIC, played live: a growling drone, a slow heartbeat and bells far away. Distant roars.
    * Water boils away down here.
    * RUINED HELLBRICK TOWERS with Hellfire Lanterns and Infernal Chests, and little shrines on the islands.
    * THE SOUL TEMPLE: one in every world, a few hundred blocks from where you land. Big, black and
      stepped, lit with lanterns... and someone is crying for help inside.

  THE DEMONS (they only live down here):
    * Lesser Demon - flies, and throws DEMON SCYTHES that speed up and fly through walls
    * Hellhound - runs you down and pounces; its bite sets you on fire
    * Cinder Imp - blinks around you and throws bouncing fireballs
    * Magma Slime - hops at you and burns; it bursts into two little ones
    * Infernal Brute - a huge slammer
    Torches and lanterns still keep them away from your base.

  NEW BLOCKS:  Brimstone, Hellstone Ore (glows; needs a diamond pickaxe or better), Scorched Ash, Hellbrick,
    Hellbrick Platform, Obsidian Bricks, Hellfire Lantern, Fireblossom (glows), Infernal Chest, Soul Cage,
    Underworld Altar, Demon Heart.
  NEW ITEMS:  Hellstone (smelt it) -> Hellstone Ingot, Demon Horn, Fireblossom Petal (eat it: lava and fire
    can't hurt you for 25 seconds), the HELLFORGED Pickaxe, Axe and Sword (the best tools in the game; the
    pickaxe breaks bedrock too, the sword sets things on fire).


THE LOST SOUL
-------------
  Caged in the Soul Temple for a thousand years. Talk to him (right-click) and FREE him.
  From then on he counts every demon you slay down here (it's shown at the top of the screen).
  At 50, go back to him: he forges you a DEMON HEART (and another for every 50 more).
  He can also tell you about the Overlord... and send you home.


THE OVERLORD  (the new boss - the hardest one in the game)
----------------------------------------------------------
  THE RITUAL (only in the Underworld): put 5 BLOCKS OF GOLD in a row in the floor, an UNDERWORLD ALTAR
  on the middle one (crafted: 2 Demon Horns, a Diamond, a Hellstone Ingot and 5 Obsidian Bricks), then the
  DEMON HEART on the altar. The gold lights up with the heartbeat... and THE OVERLORD rises out of the ground.

  A huge black DRAGON with molten scales. His long neck and tail swing as he flies, his great wings beat,
  and his jaw opens with fire glowing inside when he breathes skulls and beams at you (the fire comes out
  of his MOUTH, so watch his head). When he's enraged his horns catch fire. 3200 health (more with friends), and every hit he lands
  is 50% stronger. He NEVER stops moving: he swoops from side to side over you, fires while he flies, and
  barely pauses between attacks. His own theme: THE OVERLORD (Sounds/music/the_overlord.mp3) - drums,
  chugging guitars, a screaming lead and a choir. When he's enraged it speeds up.
    PHASE 1  Hellfire Barrage (volleys of homing skulls, fired on the move) - Demon Scythes (they fly out...
             and come BACK) - Meteor Rain (get out of the red circles) - Infernal Charge (the floor burns
             behind him) - Ground Slam (shockwaves along the floor: jump them; after a slam he's on the
             ground for a moment: hit him!) - INFERNAL BLINK (he bursts into fire, reappears above you, a
             crosshair locks on... and he dives straight through it) - Skull Storm (he circles you, raining
             skulls) - he calls demons
    PHASE 2  (65%) his horns ignite and a ring of fire blasts out: faster, double charges and double slams,
             two waves of scythes, Lava Pillars that march at you, Hell Chains (he drags you in, then slams)
             and Rings of Fire (find the gap)
    PHASE 3  (30%) ENRAGED: a wall of fire closes the arena in, DOOM SWEEPS - beams of hellfire at head height
             ("DUCK!") or at your feet ("JUMP!"), three in a row - triple charges, triple slams, double
             blinks, meteor storms.
  When he dies: he screams, light cracks out of him, explosions come faster and faster... and one huge blast
  turns the screen white (with its own death sound).
  If everyone runs away (or dies), he sinks back into the fire and leaves the Demon Heart on the altar.
  He drops the HELLFIRE CLEAVER (forged from his fang: every cut throws a crescent of hellfire that burns),
  Hellstone Ingots, Mana Stars, Diamonds, Demon Horns, Emeralds, Golden Apples and Hellfire Lanterns.


ADVANCEMENTS
------------
  Rock Bottom (make a Hellbreaker), Highway to Hell (reach the Underworld), A Soul Set Free, Heart of
  Darkness (a Demon Heart), The Ritual (call the Overlord), Overlord Overthrown, Forged in Fire (Hellforged
  Pickaxe), Back From Hell, Touchdown!
  New accessories: Overlord's Crown, Demon Horns, Soul Wisp, Ember Halo, Eye Black.


NEW MONSTER ART
---------------
  Every hostile monster except the Husk and the Cave Crawler has been redrawn: real pixel art with
  outlines and shading, built from body parts that MOVE.
  * Walkers stride and swing their arms (the Ice Husk and Crimson Husk reach for you, the Ruin Skeleton
    swings a rusty sword, the Night Stalker's arms hang to its knees, the Barkling's branches chop down).
  * The golems (Ice Golem, Magma Brute, Sentinel, Selenite Golem) and the Brutes raise both fists to slam.
  * Bats, the Gloom Owl and the demons beat real wings; bats fold them to dive.
  * The Frost Wraith and the Crimson Reaper float in rippling, tattered cloaks (the Reaper swings his scythe).
  * Wisps and the Cinder Imp's head are living flames; the Hellhound has a burning mane and tail and opens
    its jaws to bite; crawlers walk on six jointed legs; the Magma Slime squashes before it hops; the
    Sporeling crouches and springs; the Brood Larva got a new armored body.
  * Every monster is drawn on the same pixel grid as the world, so nothing looks blurry or mismatched.

MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.1.4 - THE APEX PARASITE
===========================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.1.4, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.1.4.


THE APEX PARASITE - the desert's boss
-------------------------------------
  A huge armored worm, 22 plates long, that flies through the air AND swims through the
  sand. It is fast, it twists and curls, and the less health it has left, the faster it
  gets (up to 60% faster). Touching ANY part of it hurts.

  WAKING IT
    Craft a PARASITE TOTEM: an Abyssal Heart in the middle and 8 Gold Ingots around it
    (Crafting Table):          G G G
                               G H G        G = Gold Ingot,  H = Abyssal Heart
                               G G G
    Place the totem on dry sand on the SURFACE of a DESERT, under the open sky, and
    right-click it. The ground trembles... and it bursts out from underneath.
    (Anywhere else the totem just says it only stirs in a desert.)
    If you fall - or everyone runs away - it slithers back under the sand and the
    totem is left behind for another try.

  HOW TO FIGHT IT
    * Aim for the HEAD. Every ring of its body can be hit too, but the plates blunt your
      blows. As it gets hurt it MOLTS and sheds plates, so its body takes more damage -
      but it also gets nastier.
    * PHASE 1 (100-66%)  The Hunt. It chases you in curving, weaving runs, and:
        - DIVES: climbs high above you with a roar, then plunges through where you stood.
        - BURROWS: sinks under the sand and tunnels beneath you (watch for the dust on the
          ground - that is where it will come up), then erupts under your feet, spraying
          acid into the sky.
        - SPITS ACID: circles you lobbing glowing globs of acid that arc down at you.
        - BROODS: curls up with its sacs swollen (its plates are open now - hit it!) and
          bursts into Brood Larvae.
    * PHASE 2 (66-33%)  It molts. Faster, spits two globs at a time, more larvae, and it
      CONSTRICTS: runs a ring around you that closes in. Get out through the gap!
    * PHASE 3 (below 33%)  FRENZY. Twitchy, unpredictable, double dives, triple acid and
      the most larvae.

  THE BROOD LARVAE
    Little worms of its own, each with its own way of moving:
      Guards    circle their mother like a ring, then lunge at you and fall back.
      Weavers   dart at you in a weaving line.
      Burrowers dive into the sand, tunnel under you, then leap out.
    Each has 14 health. They all vanish when the Parasite dies or leaves.

  REWARDS
    Gold Ingots, Diamonds, Emeralds, Rubies - and your Abyssal Heart back (so you only
    ever pay the 8 gold). Two new advancements: "Something Stirs Below" (wake it) and
    "Exterminator" (beat it).

  FOR TESTING
    /summon parasite     makes it rise out of the sand right where you stand
    /summon larva 5      a few Brood Larvae on their own
    Multiplayer: everyone sees and fights the same worm (it gets +50% health for each
    extra player near it, like the King - see Game Rules > Boss Scaling).


FOR WHOEVER ADDS THE NEXT BOSS
------------------------------
  The Parasite is a good example of a boss with a BODY (not just a box):
    * the boss is ONE mob (the head). parasiteSegs() builds the body from the head's path,
      so friends compute the same body themselves and only the head travels over the net.
    * parasiteProxies() hands every weapon one stand-in per ring of the body (through
      combatTargets()), and hurtMob() forwards those hits to the head.
    * its helpers (wormSteer = chase a point by turning) are reusable for any worm.
  The long comment above "THE APEX PARASITE" in index.html explains it all.
  (It plays the same boss song as the King - there is only one boss track so far.)

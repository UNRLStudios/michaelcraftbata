MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.3 - THE ADD-ONS UPDATE
==========================================

(Everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.3: the version is now beta-1.3. Worlds you already have are fine.)


ADD-ONS   (Singleplayer > Create New World > Add-ons...)
-------------------------------------------------------
  Little rule-sets, built into the game, that you pick when you make a world. They are saved with the world
  (you can't change them later), they show on the world list and the pause menu, and a friend who joins gets
  them too. Four to start with:

  NIGHTMARE MODE   Its own difficulty, far worse than Hard.
                   * ONE LIFE (it includes Hardcore)
                   * the world is ALWAYS "Moody" dark - the Brightness option is ignored
                   * monsters hit 2.5x as hard (Hard is 1.5x), have DOUBLE health and run 18% faster
                   * bosses have 50% more health and hit 1.7x as hard
                   * twice as many monsters, appearing twice as often
                   * you get hungry 50% sooner and heal less than half as fast
                   * a dark red edge closes in on the screen, and beats when you are nearly dead
  HARDCORE         ONE LIFE. When you die the world is over: you can only SPECTATE it (fly around, nothing can
                   hurt you, but you can't touch anything) or DELETE it. It is saved the moment you die, so
                   quitting can't undo it. Your hearts get a gold glow.
  MOON GRAVITY     Gravity is 60% weaker. Jumps carry you three times as high (about 4 blocks), falling is slow
                   and floaty, falls hurt far less, and landings kick up grey moon dust. Everything else is
                   light too: monsters bounce, drops drift down, particles float.
  SPACE DETENTION  You are stuck on this world, and the only way out is up. See below.


SPACE DETENTION: BUILD A ROCKET AND ESCAPE
------------------------------------------
  1. Craft a LAUNCH PLATFORM (Crafting Table): 3 Iron Ingots, a Block of Coal, 3 Cobblestone.
  2. Hold it: a glowing hologram of the launch site and the rocket follows your cursor (it turns red when the
     ground is wrong). Right-click to place it. It needs open sky and flat-ish ground; it CLEARS the site
     (trees, hills and all), lays a 5-wide pad and fills in any gap under it.
  3. Now the transparent BLUEPRINT hangs over the pad: a faint grid, a scan line, and every missing part as
     a glowing ghost. Hold a part and its ghost cells pulse. A checklist on the left tells you what is missing.
  4. Craft the parts and place each one on ITS cell (a part refuses any other spot, and tells you what the
     blueprint wants there). 25 parts in all:
        Rocket Engine x3     3 Ember Ingots + 3 Iron + 2 Obsidian each                    (Ashlands)
        Rocket Fuel Tank x9  3 Frost Crystals + 4 Iron makes 3                              (Frostlands)
        Rocket Hull x9       8 Iron makes 4
        Rocket Fin L / R     3 Redwood Ingots + 1 Iron each (two different recipes)      (Redwood forest)
        Rocket Cockpit x1    3 Glass + Diamond + 2 Ancient Metal + Iron                      (Ancient Ruins!)
        Rocket Nose Cone x1  4 Gold + Ruby + 3 Iron
     (Engines, Tanks, Fins and the Cockpit need all three new biomes, and the Cockpit's Ancient Metal comes
     from the Hollow Knight, which needs the Apex Parasite's key: so a rocket takes a whole playthrough.)
  5. When every cell is filled the blueprint turns green. RIGHT-CLICK THE COCKPIT: countdown from 5, the engines
     light, the ground shakes, LIFTOFF - the rocket climbs through the sky into black space and stars.
  6. "YOU ESCAPED!": keep playing (you get 2 Mana Stars and 8 Diamonds, plus the Astronaut Helmet accessory) or
     save and quit. Break the middle platform to pack the launch site up and get your Launch Platform back.
  The parts only craft (and the platform only places) in Space Detention worlds. Only the host can launch.
  New advancements: Launch Site, Ready for Liftoff, Escape Velocity.


MANA STARS  (a bigger mana bar, not a faster one)
-------------------------------------------------
  Eat a MANA STAR: +20 maximum mana, for good, up to 9 stars (100 -> 280). Mana still refills at the same speed,
  so a bigger bar means more spells before you run dry. They are saved with you, and the bar grows wider
  and shows a star for each one you have.
  Where: 1-2 from the Hollow Knight, 1 from the Apex Parasite, 2 from the King of the Abyss, 1 from the
  Crimson Reaper (70%), sometimes in Ancient Ruins chests, or CRAFT one: 2 Moonpetals + Frost Crystal + Diamond.


NEW AND CHANGED WEAPONS
-----------------------
  SHORT BOW   Sticks + String + an Iron Ingot. HOLD RIGHT-CLICK and it looses an arrow every 0.3 seconds
              (a bit wild, a bit weaker than a long draw: arrows are used up fast!). Left-click still draws it like
              a normal bow. Smaller in your hands, too.
  FROST BLADE Every cut now throws a shard of frost (55% of the sword's damage, short range, chills what it hits).


ARMOR IS A LITTLE WEAKER (everything, in every way)
---------------------------------------------------
                        was -> now
  Leather Tunic          3 -> 2 armor
  Iron Chestplate        6 -> 5
  Cobalt Chestplate      7 -> 6, breath lasts 1.5x (was 2x), swims a bit slower
  Diamond Chestplate     9 -> 8
  Ruby Chestplate       10 -> 9
  Samurai Armor          9 -> 8, katana bonus +1 damage (was +2), dash cooldown -15% (was -25%)
  Parasite Armor        11 -> 9, +0.5 damage (was +1), +1.5 hearts (was +2), leech 3% (was 4%)
  Abyssal Armor         13 -> 11, +4 damage (was +5), +2.5 hearts (was +3)
  Ancient Armor         12 -> 10, +1.5 damage (was +2), +1.5 hearts (was +2), thorns 15% (was 25%)
  Ancient Armor now REALLY hurts back whatever bites you (it was only written on the tooltip before).

MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.1.5 - SPRINKLERS
====================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.1.5, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.1.5.

Farming is easier now. Like the Gas Furnace, sprinklers are a system you build in the world,
Create-style, out of three new blocks:

  WATER PUMP   the tank. 6 iron ingots, 2 glass and a Bucket:      I I I
                                                                   G B G
                                                                   I I I
  WATER PIPE   4 pipes from 2 iron ingots and a glass:             I G I
  SPRINKLER    2 sprinklers from 3 iron ingots and a pipe:         I P I
                                                                     I

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Place a Water Pump and RIGHT-CLICK it. Put Water Buckets in the slot on the left
     (or just right-click it WITH a Water Bucket in your hand): each one fills the tank by
     100 units and you get the empty Bucket back out of the slot on the right. The tank holds
     16 buckets.
  2. Run Water Pipes away from the pump, touching it on any side, in any shape you like:
     up, down, round corners, through walls (up to 96 pipes). Pipes join up to whatever they
     touch, with flanges at every joint. They light up aqua with running water when the pump
     has some.
  3. Put Sprinklers on the pipes (a sprinkler is a pipe joint too, so pipe - sprinkler -
     pipe - sprinkler works). EACH PUMP FEEDS UP TO 10 SPRINKLERS (the nearest ten): any
     others on the same pump get nothing and show a red mark.

WHAT A SPRINKLER DOES
  It waters a patch 7 blocks wide: 3 blocks to its left, 3 to its right, and itself, from one
  block above it to two blocks below. Put it right next to your farmland (or on a pipe
  running along the top of your field). Watered farmland goes dark and shiny, and crops
  growing in it grow about 3x faster. Water sprays out in arcs and the nozzle spins.
  A sprinkler only drinks while it has something to water (0.4 units a second), and an empty
  tank waters nothing: the pump screen shows the tank, how many sprinklers are connected,
  how many are watering right now, and tells you what's wrong.

ALSO
  * Breaking the pump spills the buckets inside it (the water in the tank is lost).
  * Multiplayer: everyone sees the sprays, the wet farmland and the tank level; one person
    uses the pump screen at a time, like a chest.
  * New advancement: "Let It Rain" (get a sprinkler watering your crops).
  * Everything from 1.1.4 (see WHATS_NEW_BETA_1.1.4.txt) is still here.

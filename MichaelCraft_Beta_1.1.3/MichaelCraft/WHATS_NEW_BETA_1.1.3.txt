MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.1.3 - SKINS
===============================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.1.3, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.1.3.


SKINS (title screen > Skins, or Pause > Skins)
----------------------------------------------
  Your look is yours, not a world's: it's saved in your browser and follows you into
  every world. Friends in multiplayer see it (colours, styles and accessories).

  PRESETS (16): Miner (the old look), Explorer, Knight, Ninja, Farmer, Pirate,
  Scientist, Abyss Walker, Sakura, Diver, Lumberjack, Nomad, Frost, Volcano,
  Bald Brute, Royal. Click one to wear it.

  CUSTOM: pick colours for your Skin, Hair, Eyes, Shirt, Pants and Shoes from the
  swatches - or ANY colour with the picker at the end of each row. Plus:
    Hair styles:  Short, Long, Buzz, Spiky, Ponytail, Bald
    Shirts:       T-Shirt, Long Sleeves, Tank Top, Stripes
  (Armour still covers your shirt and pants while you wear it.)

  The big preview walks (pause it with the button under it) and turns around.
  Your inventory shows your real look now too, accessories and all.


ACCESSORIES: 55, each EARNED by a different advancement
-------------------------------------------------------------

  the advancement that gives it, and once earned it's yours in every world
  (advancements you already got in your old worlds count as soon as you load them).
  Locked ones show a lock - hover one to see which advancement earns it.
  Some glow (the Crown of the Abyss, the Halo, the Miner's Helmet lamp...).

MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.0.2 - THE MULTIPLAYER FIX-UP (AND GOLD!)
============================================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.0.2, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.0.2.
Your worlds from 1.0.1 load fine (they start with the default game rules).


MULTIPLAYER BUG FIXES
---------------------
No more duping from furnaces, gem cutters or chests
  - Spam-clicking the output of a furnace or gem cutter used to give you extra items.
    Before, the host and each friend had their own copy of every container and sent
    it back and forth. Whichever copy arrived last won, so a click could get "undone"
    and the item came back.
  - Now only ONE player can have a container open at a time. If someone else is
    using it you'll see "Mikey is using that." Whoever has it open is the only one
    who can change it, and the host always has the real contents.
  - Shift-clicking fuel in or out no longer makes it disappear (same cause, same fix).
  - Breaking a chest/furnace: only the host spills what's inside, so it can't drop
    twice. You can't break one while someone else has it open.
  - Supply chests are only filled with loot once, by the host. Chests inside
    buildings (temples etc.) are shared too, so leaving and rejoining can't refill them.
  - This works for every container, including any new ones added later.

The King of the Abyss works for everyone
  - The real bug: the King has an internal timer called "t", and the network used
    "t" for the monster's TYPE, so friends' games didn't know what he was and threw
    him away. Fixed for every monster.
  - He now goes after whoever is closest (and switches targets now and then), not
    just the host. His fireballs, orbs, meteors, claws and charge hurt everyone.
  - Friends see his projectiles, hear his sounds, feel the screen shake, and get the
    boss bar and the Final Stand music.
  - Anyone can summon him at the altar, or with /summon king (cheats on).
  - He only leaves when EVERY player nearby is dead or has run away. The host dying
    doesn't end the fight any more.
  - Everyone who fought him gets the "king" advancement; whoever summoned him gets
    the summon one.
  - Placing the altar away from an Abyss no longer teleports him to a far-away Abyss.
  - Any future boss gets all of this automatically (see "BOSSES" in index.html).

Much less lag
  - The host used to keep throwing away and rebuilding the world around one player
    to make room for another when you were far apart. Now it keeps the world loaded
    around everyone.
  - Monster updates now only send what changed (about a quarter of the data now).
  - During the boss fight, the King's whole arena map was being sent 8 times a
    second. Not any more.
  - Monsters, furnaces and drops were running at double speed while the host was
    dead or in a menu. Fixed.
  - The inventory screen no longer redraws every time some furnace somewhere ticks.
  - Reading blocks is faster in general, for single player too.


GAME RULES  (Pause > Game Rules, or "Game Rules..." when creating a world)
----------
  Keep Inventory ........... ON: keep your stuff when you die. OFF: drop it all where you fell.
  Friendly Fire (PvP) ...... players can hurt each other with any weapon (default OFF).
  Mob Spawning ............. monsters and animals keep appearing.
  Daylight Cycle ........... OFF: the time of day freezes.
  Natural Regeneration ..... heal slowly when your hunger bar is nearly full.
  Hunger ................... OFF: nobody gets hungry.
  Fall Damage .............. falling from high up hurts.
  Bosses Scale With Players  a boss gets +50% health for every extra player near it.
  Death Messages ........... everyone sees "Joiny was slain by Mikey." etc.
  Friends Can Use Commands . with cheats on, friends can use commands too.

  - The host's rules count for everyone. Friends can look at them but not change them.
  - Command: /gamerule to list them, /gamerule keepInventory off to change one.
  - Rules are saved with each world.


GOLD IS USEFUL NOW
------------------
  Gold tools (pick, axe, shovel, sword): the FASTEST mining in the game, but they
    wear out quickly. They're lucky:
      - Gold Pickaxe: 30% chance of a second drop from any ore.
      - Gold Sword: monsters you finish off drop double loot half the time.
  Golden Apple (8 gold ingots around an apple): heals 4 hearts at once, then keeps
    healing for 6 seconds. You can eat it even when you're full. Perfect for bosses.
  Villagers: Toolsmiths buy 5 gold ingots for an emerald. Farmers sell Golden Apples.
  New advancement: "Midas Touch".

                                   G . G      G = Gold Ingot

FOR THE CURIOUS (how the multiplayer fixes work)
------------------------------------------------
  Containers: the host keeps a list of who has each container open (a "lock").
  Opening one asks the host first; the host says yes (with the real contents) or no.
  While you have it open, only your game changes it and runs its furnace, and every
  change goes to the host right away, followed by your inventory, in that order, so
  the two always match even if you quit suddenly.

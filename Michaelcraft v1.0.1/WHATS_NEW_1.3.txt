MICHAELCRAFT ALPHA 1.3 - THE COMBAT UPDATE
===========================================

NEW WEAPONS
-----------
Katana            Iron Stick + String + 2 Iron Ingots (crafting table, diagonal: blade top-right,
                  stick bottom-left, string next to it)
                  - Left-click: 3-hit combo (falling cut, rising cut, lunging thrust that hits harder)
                  - Right-click: IAIDO dash. You dash ~5 blocks and can't be hurt mid-dash;
                    a heartbeat later everything you passed gets cut.
Sensei's Katana   Won by beating a Sensei in a duel. Faster, longer dash, red trail,
                  cherry petals, and cuts make enemies bleed.
Sledgehammer      6 Blocks of Iron (top two rows) + Iron Stick (bottom middle).
                  32x32 texture so it's HUGE in your hands.
                  - Left-click: wind up and SMASH. It's AREA damage: every creature in a circle
                    around the impact gets hit, and the shockwave runs along the ground both
                    ways (so monsters behind you get it too, at 60%). Blocks pop up in a
                    wave, with Minecraft-style square dust and block chips.
                    Monsters get flattened, knocked flying and see stars.
                    Finish one off and it gets sent into orbit.
                  - Right-click: GROUND POUND (jump, flip, slam down on everyone around).
                  - It's heavy: you walk a bit slower holding it.
Tonio's Musket    Only ONE per world. Tonio sells it for 60 emeralds.
                  - Left-click: fire. Then it reloads itself (about 2 seconds) using a Musket Ball.
                  - Shots PIERCE: the ball goes through up to 4 monsters in a line
                    (each one takes 25% less).
                  - The top quarter of a monster is its head: HEADSHOTS do 1.75x damage.
                  - New detailed 32x32 flintlock model (and its own side-on model in your hands).
                  - Right-click to reload by hand.

SAMURAI ARMOR (your texture)
-------------
Buy it from any Sensei: 45 emeralds, or 30 once you've beaten that Sensei.
7 armor (like diamond). With a katana in hand: +2 damage and a 25% shorter dash cooldown.
When you wear it you look the part: red lacquered plates laced with black silk, gold trim.
Your new iron, diamond and ruby chestplate textures are in too (same file names as before).

NEW ITEMS
---------
Iron Stick        2 Iron Ingots stacked -> 2 Iron Sticks
String            Cave Crawlers drop it, 1 Leather -> 2 String, or buy it from the Fisherman
Musket Balls      Iron Ingot + Coal + Paper -> 8 (or 12 for 2 emeralds from Tonio)
Tonio's Map       Map with a Gold Ingot on each side. Cartographers sell it too.
Dojo Map          Map with a Coal on each side. Cartographers sell it too.
                  Right-click a locator map to mark the place on your world map;
                  hold it and an arrow points the way.

TONIO
-----
(Redrawn: round curly afro, sideburns, mustache and goatee, red bandana, leather vest,
 bandolier of brass musket balls, rolled sleeves, tall boots. He gets a face-on portrait
 when you talk to him.)
A log cabin somewhere a few hundred blocks from spawn (only one per world). Go in with W.
Tonio (big black afro, mustache, old musketeer coat) greets you by name.
Right-click him to talk: buy Bessie the musket, buy musket balls, hear some stories.
Once you buy it, the musket rack on his wall stays empty.

THE DOJOS AND THE SENSEIS
-------------------------
Exactly three dojos in every world: a two-storey Japanese hall on a stone base, with a torii
gate, stone lanterns, a gravel path and cherry trees dropping petals. It's built from real
blocks (your base/pillar/lattice textures + new kawara roof tiles), so you can walk in,
jump up onto the beams, and take it apart for building. There's a chest upstairs.

Each has a Sensei: Kenji the Swift, Hiroshi the Still and Takeshi the Storm.
Right-click to talk (lore about Master Masamura and the three crimson blades forged from
the heart of the Abyss) or to challenge him to a duel for his sword.

THE DUEL (mini-boss, with its own note-block duel music)
  - Combo: he raises his blade before each cut, then has to catch his breath.
  - Iaido dash: he crouches with his hand on the hilt and a red line shows his path.
    JUMP over it (or dash through it with your own katana).
  - Counter stance: he glows BLUE with a blue "!" - DON'T hit him, or he parries and cuts back.
  - Leap (Takeshi, or anyone at half health): jumps, hangs, and crashes down on the red marker.
  - Shuriken (half health): three throwing stars in a fan.
  - After a combo, a dash or a leap he's open for a moment: hits then do +25%.
  - At half health he gets serious: faster, red eyes, faster music.
  - If you die he heals and waits for you. If you run off (48+ blocks) the duel ends.
  - Beat him: Sensei's Katana + emeralds + string. After that you can spar again for fun.

TALKING TO NPCS
---------------
Right-click Tonio or a Sensei. The line types out (click it, or press Space/Enter, to skip),
then pick a choice with the mouse or keys 1-9. Esc leaves. Chrome's right-click menu no
longer pops up, and the click that opens the talk screen can't press a choice by accident.

MENU FIXES
----------
- "Allow Cheats" when you create a world. With cheats OFF there are no commands
  (except /help, /pos and /seed) and you can't switch game modes from the pause menu.
  Picking Creative turns cheats on (like Minecraft), but you can turn it back off.
- Worlds made before 1.3 keep cheats ON, so you don't lose your commands.
- Usernames: set yours when you make a world (or in Options > Gameplay). It shows over your
  head (you can turn that off), in death messages, and people call you by it.

MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.1 - THE GAS FURNACE UPDATE
==============================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.1, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.1.
Older worlds load fine.


THE GAS FURNACE (built in the world, Create-style)
--------------------------------------------------
  It isn't one recipe: you craft three parts and BUILD it in the world.

  1. GAS FURNACE (Crafting Table)    I I I      I = Iron Ingot
                                     I F I      F = Furnace
                                     C C C      C = Cobblestone
  2. EXHAUST PIPES x6 (Crafting Table): three Iron Ingots in a row
  3. VENTS x2 (Crafting Table)       I S I      S = Stick
                                     S . S
                                     I S I

  Build it: put the Gas Furnace down, run Exhaust Pipes away from it (touching it
  on any side), and finish with a Vent that has open air on one side for the smoke.

  - Pipes join up on their own to whatever they touch (pipes, the furnace, vents),
    with copper flanges at every joint and chunky boxes at bends and branches.
  - Make it any shape you like: a chimney straight up, along a ceiling, round
    corners, through walls, branching off - up to 64 pipes.
  - MORE VENTS = FASTER. One vent: smelts 3x as fast as a Furnace. Two: 4x.
    Three or more: 5x. Branch the pipe and cap each branch with a vent.
  - It burns fuel only 1.5x as fast as a Furnace, so every item costs less fuel.
  - The smoke comes out of each vent's open side (straight on if it can, else up).
  - Two Gas Furnaces can share the same pipes and vents.
  - It re-checks its pipes twice a second, so you can rebuild while it runs.

  You can SEE it working (no text anywhere):
  - running: the window glows, it lights up the room, heat pulses run down the
    pipes toward the vents, the vent fans spin and puff smoke out, and it roars
    (blast furnace sounds)
  - no working vent: it won't light, and black smoke chokes out of its top
  - its screen has three fan lights: lit for each working vent (up to 3), a red X
    when there are none

  New advancement: "Industrial Revolution" - get a Gas Furnace venting.
  Textures (16x16, swap in your own if you like): Textures/blocks/gas_furnace.png,
  gas_furnace_on.png, vent.png, vent_base.png, exhaust_pipe.png (the pipe's icon -
  in the world pipes are drawn joined up).


REGENERATION CHARM (new charm, tiers I to VIII)
-----------------------------------------------
  Recipe (any grid, shapeless): Gold Charm Frame + 2 Golden Apples + a Ruby.
  Heals you all the time, even when you're hungry:
     I     II    III   IV    V     VI    VII   VIII
     8s    6.5s  5.2s  4.2s  3.4s  2.7s  2.2s  1.8s   per heart
  Little hearts float up as it heals. Merge two at a Charm Merger for the next
  tier, like any charm.


NO MORE TIP TEXT IN BLOCK SCREENS
---------------------------------
  The Furnace, Gem Cutter, Enchanting Table, Charm Merger and Crafting Table screens
  no longer tell you what to do: the help paragraphs, the labels under the slots
  ("Coal Block", "Raw Iron", "Item", "Charm"...), the Gem Cutter's status line and
  the hover hints are all gone. Just the slots, like Minecraft.


PLAY IT ON YOUR PHONE (install it as an app)
--------------------------------------------
  - The game can now be installed on a phone like an app, with its own icon,
    full screen and sideways - and it works OFFLINE after the first load.
    See HOW_TO_PLAY_ON_YOUR_PHONE.txt (it has to be on a website for this: free
    and quick with Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages).
  - New files: manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icons/ (a grass block + a diamond
    pickaxe icon).
  - "Install App" shows on the title screen when the browser can install it
    (Chrome on Android, desktop Chrome/Edge). iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen.

PHONE FIXES
  - Title screen: the logo no longer gets cut off on a phone held sideways.
  - Inventory and every block screen fit on a sideways phone without scrolling:
    your inventory moves to a second column on the right.
  - New DOWN button on the touch controls (drop through platforms, dive while
    flying, climb down ladders) - and the jump button now climbs ladders.
  - The first tap on a menu button goes full screen and turns sideways (when the
    browser allows it).
  - No more accidental zooming, page bouncing or text selection while playing.
  - The corner text (day, X, biome) no longer runs under the buttons.
  - Phones start on the lighter "Performance" resolution for a smoother frame rate
    (Options > Video > Resolution to change it).

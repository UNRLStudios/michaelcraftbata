MichaelCraft v1.0 - THE FULL RELEASE
An UNRL Studios game. Michael Muth Jr. (game developer), Claude Opus 5.5 (co-developer),
Cash Sklare (alpha & beta tester).

THE PATH (the bosses unlock in order now; no tool requirements, just the order)
  1. Get at least full diamond gear (pickaxe, axe, sword, chestplate).
  2. Defeat the Apex Parasite (desert).
  3. Defeat the Hollow Knight (Ancient Ruins). Until he falls, the King's altar stays cold.
  4. Defeat the King of the Abyss. Until he falls, the bedrock will NOT crack (even with a Hellbreaker).
     By now you should have Abyssal Armor and a good sword like the Parasite Katana.
  5. Break into the Underworld, find and free the Lost Soul, slay 50 demons.
  6. Defeat THE OVERLORD.
  7. Wake the totem (the King), build a Supercharger, gear up.
  8. Defeat the Slayer of Worlds. The credits roll... and HARDMODE begins.

THE GUIDE (press G, or Pause > Guide)
  Every step from your first tree to Hardmode, with tips. Steps tick themselves off as you go, and the
  next one is highlighted. The key can be changed in Options > Controls.
  New players get a welcome card the first time they start a world.

TUTORIAL WORLD (title screen)
  Its own little world, always the same, never saved. 14 short steps: walk, jump, chop, craft planks,
  a crafting table, a pickaxe, mine, ore, torches, eating, fighting, and the Guide.

THE SLAYER OF WORLDS
  Four times as deadly as 1.7, and 10,000 health. She hits for roughly 190-280 before armor.
  You will need supercharged armor (and to dodge).
  She drops the totem again when she falls, so you can keep supercharging.

ENCHANTING goes up to V
  Costs: I 8, II 16, III 28, IV 45 emeralds + 2 diamonds, V 70 emeralds + 5 diamonds.
  /enchant works up to 5.

THE SUPERCHARGER (new block)
  Recipe: Hellstone Ingot, Void Crystal, Hellstone Ingot / Obsidian Bricks, Enchanting Table, Obsidian Bricks /
  Obsidian Bricks, Block of Diamond, Obsidian Bricks.
  Right-click it with the AWAKENED totem to set the totem on top. Then right-click it to open it.
  Each roll costs 8 emeralds + 1 diamond and gives one random power (a new roll replaces the old one):
    Weapons: Damage (+v% damage), Leech (heals you), Fire Aspect (burns, extra damage)
    Armor:   Absorption (every hit divided by 1 + v%), Armor Points (+v% of the armor's points)
  v goes from +10% (1 in 2) to +400% (1 in 500,000):
    +50% 1 in 7 / +100% 1 in 35 / +200% 1 in 853 / +300% 1 in 20,648 / +400% 1 in 500,000
  Rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, MYTHIC. Supercharged items glow in their rarity color.
  Duck (C) + right-click the Supercharger to take the totem back (you need it for her altar).
  The King of the Abyss tells you about it now (ask him "Can the totem do anything else?").

CREDITS
  Roll after the Slayer of Worlds falls (skippable). Also: the Credits button on the title screen, and /credits.

HARDMODE (after the Slayer)
  - Monsters have 60% more health and hit 50% harder, and there are more of them.
  - Bosses have 60% more health, hit 40% harder, and drop Voidsteel Ingots.
  - Bands of BLIGHT across the land (not near spawn): purple soil, Blightstone, dead trees, a purple haze.
  - New monster: the VOID WRAITH (flies, fires void bolts). Corrupted Shades roam at night and in the Blight.
  - VOIDSTEEL ORE deep underground (needs a Hellforged pickaxe). Smelt it into ingots.
    Voidsteel Pickaxe / Axe / Shovel / Sword (the best tools), Voidsteel Armor (Abyssal Armor + 7 ingots):
    14 armor, +5 damage, +4 hearts.
  - /hardmode on|off (cheats).

SETTINGS AND PERFORMANCE (Options > Video)
  - Boss Effects: Full / Reduced / Minimal (screen tears, lensing, flashes and particle counts).
  - Mob Detail: High / Low (Low draws monsters faster).
  - Presets: Best Looks, Balanced, Max Performance (one click).
  - Phones start on Reduced boss effects and Low mob detail.

CHUNKS AND LOADING
  The world is made in chunks (16 blocks wide). Before, every chunk was built the moment anything looked
  at it, so teleporting, flying fast or zooming out could freeze the game while several were built at once.
  - The chunk under you is made right away, the ones on screen next (one a frame), and the rest of the
    Loaded Chunks are made in the spare moments between frames. Land that isn't ready yet hides in fog
    (with "Loading...") instead of freezing the game.
  - Options > Video > World:
      Loaded Chunks (3-12, default 5): how far around you the world is kept and keeps running.
      Render Chunks (2-12, default 6): how far you can see before the fog.
    The presets set them too (Max Performance: 3 / 3, Best Looks: 7 / 10). Phones start on 4 / 4.
  - A loading screen when a world opens: the land around you, chunk by chunk (nearest first), then the
    light, then the creatures, with a progress bar and a tip.
  - /chunks shows how many are loaded.

FIXES BEFORE RELEASE
  - Bosses only did 1 damage to anyone in good armor (armor took a flat amount off every hit). Now, against
    a boss (and anything hitting you during a boss fight), armor blocks a SHARE of each hit instead:
    10 armor blocks 29%, 16 (Abyssal + Protection V) blocks 39%, 20 blocks 44%, 50 blocks 67%.
    Armor tooltips show it ("Blocks 39% of a boss's damage"). Normal monsters work the same as before.
  - The game no longer pauses when you open your inventory: monsters keep coming, you keep falling,
    furnaces keep smelting, the clock keeps ticking (you just stand still while it's open).
  - Voidsteel Armor shows on your character now: dark violet plates with glowing cyan seams, full
    gauntlets and greaves (like the other armors, the head stays yours).
  - The Supercharger has new art (an arcane forge: two claws hold the totem over a glowing chamber), and
    it's a background block like the Crafting Table and Enchanting Table (you walk in front of it).
  - The Creative inventory is sorted: tabs for Building, Nature, Utility, Tools, Combat, Food, Items and
    Eggs, and inside each one, headed groups where things that belong together sit together (ores in
    order, tools tier by tier from wood to Voidsteel, fish raw then cooked, every biome's blocks together).
  - A Void Wraith spawn egg.
  - The title menu is bigger (it grows with your screen), and Options sits next to Credits;
    Tutorial World moved to the bottom row between Skins and Exit Game.

OTHER
  - Hellforged tools (pickaxe, axe, sword, Hellbreaker, Hellfire Cleaver) are animated: molten metal
    ripples along them, in your hand and in every slot.
  - New arrow textures (arrow, iron, diamond, ruby).
  - Parasite gear nerfed: tools mine at 10.5 (was 12.5), 600 uses, 8 damage; less leech; armor 8 defense,
    +1 heart; Parasite Katana 11 damage. The Apex Parasite drops 5 ingots (was 20).
  - FIX: the Underworld's progress (Lost Soul, demons, the Overlord) and the Slayer's flags were not kept
    when you reloaded a world. They are now.

PLAYING MICHAELCRAFT ON YOUR PHONE (as an app)
==============================================

Phones only let you install a web game as an app when it's on a WEBSITE (https).
Opening index.html straight from a folder on your phone works for playing, but it
can't be installed and won't have an icon. So, once:

1. PUT THE GAME ONLINE (free, about 2 minutes)
   Easiest: Netlify Drop
     - on a computer, go to  app.netlify.com/drop
     - drag the whole "MichaelCraft" folder (the one with index.html in it) onto the page
     - it gives you a link like  https://something-random.netlify.app
       (make a free account to keep it and give it a nicer name)
   Or GitHub Pages: put the MichaelCraft folder in a GitHub repository and turn on
   Pages in its Settings.

2. INSTALL IT
   Android (Chrome): open the link. Tap "Install App" on the title screen
                     (or Chrome's menu > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen").
   iPhone (Safari):  open the link, tap Share (the square with the arrow),
                     then "Add to Home Screen".

3. PLAY
   Tap the MichaelCraft icon. It opens full screen, sideways, like a real app.
   After the first time it loads, it works OFFLINE too (it keeps a copy of
   everything on your phone).

UPDATING
   Upload the new version to the same place (drag the new folder onto Netlify
   again). For phones to pick it up straight away, change VERSION at the top of
   sw.js (for example to 'michaelcraft-beta-1.2') every time you upload.

FILES THAT MAKE THIS WORK (keep them next to index.html)
   manifest.webmanifest  - the app's name, icon, full screen + sideways
   sw.js                 - the offline copy
   icons/                - the app icon (a grass block and a diamond pickaxe; swap in
                           your own 192x192 and 512x512 PNGs if you like)

Multiplayer still needs an internet connection.

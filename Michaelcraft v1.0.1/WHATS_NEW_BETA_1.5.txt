MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.5 - THE SKY ISLANDS
=======================================

(Everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.5: the version is now beta-1.5. Worlds you already have are fine:
 they keep their land exactly as it was. The plains spawn and the Sky Islands come with NEW worlds.)


THE SKY ISLANDS  (a new, very rare biome)
-----------------------------------------
  * Clusters of floating islands, high above a quiet meadow: grass, flowers, cherry, oak and birch trees,
    and stone underneath with a little coal, iron, gold and even diamond in it.
  * They're RARE: about one cluster every 7,000 blocks, and never close to the spawn.
  * Getting up: look for a trail of blue GUST CLOUDS rising from the ground. Land on one and it throws you
    high into the air (hold Duck or Down to stand on one without bouncing). They break your fall, too.
  * White CLOUDS float between the islands: stand on them, and jump up through them from below.
  * New blocks: Cloud and Gust Cloud.
  * New advancements: Head in the Clouds (set foot on the Sky Islands) and Updraft (ride a Gust Cloud).


MICHAEL
-------
  * Michael lives in a little cottage on the biggest Sky Island. He made this whole world, and he has a lot
    to say: right-click him to talk (who he is, how the clouds work, tips, and more).
  * He runs a shop where everything is REALLY cheap: diamonds, iron, gold, golden apples, mana potions,
    mana stars, food, arrows, clouds... and he'll even swap you an emerald for some coal.
  * He sells his own invention: the MAGMA CANNON (12 emeralds).
  * New advancement: Meet the Maker.


THE MAGMA CANNON
----------------
  * A magic weapon (it costs mana: 22 a shot). Left-click to fire.
  * It lobs a spinning ball of molten rock that trails fire, smoke and sparks, then bursts into a LAVA STORM:
    a double shockwave, a blinding flash, flying lava, five burning globs that burst again where they land,
    and pools of lava on the ground that burn any monster standing in them. Everything it hits catches fire.
  * You hold it like a real cannon (aimed with the mouse), its core glows, and it kicks you back when it
    fires (fire it at the ground in mid-air for a little lift).
  * Friends in multiplayer see the whole show.
  * New advancement: Lava Storm.


A BIG PLAINS SPAWN
------------------
  * Every new world now starts in a wide, open plains (about 220 blocks across), with gentle hills.


BUSIER NIGHTS
-------------
  * Monsters spawn a LOT more at night: about twice as many, and much faster. Light up your base!
  * The biome monsters (Redwood, Frostlands, Ashlands) come out in bigger numbers at night too.


SPAWN EGGS  (Creative only)
---------------------------
  * A spawn egg for every creature (40 of them), in the new EGGS tab of the Creative inventory.
  * Right-click to spawn the creature where your cursor is. In Survival they don't work.
  * In multiplayer, the host spawns it for you.


MORE KEYS YOU CAN CHANGE
------------------------
  * Options > Controls now has: Hotbar Slot 1 to 9, Next Hotbar Slot, Previous Hotbar Slot (both unset at
    first), Pause / Back, and Open Command (/).
  * While a key is flashing, press DELETE to clear it. Esc always works as Back, whatever you set.


SMOOTHER MONSTERS
-----------------
  * The monsters are drawn at your screen's full resolution now, so they glide and their arms, legs and
    wings turn smoothly, instead of moving in big pixel steps.


NEW COMMANDS
------------
  * /tp and /locate now know every biome: plains, forest, desert, taiga, mountains, ocean, snow (plus
    redwood, frost and ash, as before).
  * /tp skyislands (or /tp sky) and /locate skyislands find the nearest Sky Islands.
  * /tp michael takes you right to Michael's door.

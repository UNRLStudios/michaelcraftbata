MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.2 - THE WILDS UPDATE
========================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.2, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.2.
WORLDS MADE BEFORE 1.2 keep their old land (so your buildings don't end up floating): they get all the new
ITEMS, MONSTERS, FISHING, MAGIC, NIGHTS and the BLOOD MOON, but not the new biomes or the Ancient Ruins.
Make a NEW WORLD to explore those.


THREE NEW BIOMES  (/locate redwood|frost|ash, then /tp redwood|frost|ash)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  REDWOOD FOREST   Giant redwoods, 3-4 blocks thick and 28-50 tall, with branches and a dark green crown.
                   Dark red-brown grass, ferns, mushrooms (the red ones are poisonous), fallen logs,
                   and small abandoned CABINS (cobwebs, a chest). REDWOOD ORE hides in the rock: smelt
                   Raw Redwood into Redwood Ingots for Redwood tools (better than iron).
                   Sounds: birds by day, owls and creaking trunks at night, wind in the needles.
                   Monsters: BARKLING (looks like a stump until you step close!), SPORELING (hops at you;
                   its spores POISON), GLOOM OWL (night).
  FROSTLANDS       The snowy biome, now frozen all the way down: Frost Stone, ICE CAVES lined with Blue Ice,
                   hanging ICICLES (walk under one and it DROPS on you), Frost Crystals that glow, FROZEN
                   CHESTS with ice weapons, and FROST ORE (Frost Crystals). An aurora dances at night.
                   Monsters: ICE HUSK (its hits chill you), FROST WRAITH (throws ice shards), ICE GOLEM
                   (rare, in the ice caves, slams the ground).
                   New ice gear: FROST BLADE (chills what it cuts), GLACIER HAMMER (freezes monsters solid),
                   and the Frost Wand (magic).
  ASHLANDS         Black volcanic land under a smoky sky, with falling ash and rising embers. Ash Blocks,
                   Ashen Stone, VOLCANOES with lava craters, lava lakes, BURNING TREES (don't touch), cinder
                   weeds. Underground it is EXTREMELY dangerous: huge caverns, lava hiding inside the rock,
                   and every cave deeper than Y 112 is flooded with lava. EMBER ORE gives Raw Ember -> Ember
                   Ingots -> the Ember Pickaxe (smelts ore as you mine!), Ember Sword (sets things alight),
                   Ember Staff and Lightning Rod.
                   Monsters: EMBER WISP (shoots fireballs), CINDER CRAWLER (sets you alight), MAGMA BRUTE.
  Each biome shows its name when you walk in, has its own sky, particles and sounds, and unlocks a
  cosmetic accessory with its advancement.


FISHING
-------
  Fishing Rod: 3 sticks + 2 string (see the recipe book). RIGHT-CLICK to cast, wait for the bobber to
  dip and a "!" to pop up, then RIGHT-CLICK again to reel in. In the Ashlands you can fish in LAVA.
    Forest and lakes   Bass, Trout
    Frostlands         Icefish, Frost Koi
    Ashlands           Magmafish
    The sea            Tuna, Pufferfish, and rarely a Shark
  Now and then you reel in treasure (or junk). Eat them raw (a little food - Magmafish burn you and the
  Pufferfish poisons you!) or COOK them in a furnace for real meals with a bonus:
    Cooked Trout swift  - Cooked Icefish frostproof - Cooked Frost Koi regeneration - Cooked Magmafish
    FIREPROOF (lava and fire can't hurt you!) - Cooked Tuna strength - Cooked Shark heals and strength -
    Cooked Pufferfish water breathing.
  Butchers buy fish for emeralds and sell Fishing Rods.
  New status effects show on the right of the screen: Poisoned, Chilled, Swift, Strong, Fireproof,
  Frostproof, Water Breathing, Regeneration.


MAGIC AND THE MANA BAR
----------------------
  The blue MANA bar appears above your hotbar when you hold a magic weapon (or when it isn't full).
  Casting costs mana, not durability. It refills by itself (about 5 a second after a short pause) and from
  MANA POTIONS (2 Glass Bottles + Moonpetal + Frost Crystal -> 2 potions).  LEFT-CLICK (hold) to cast.
    Ember Staff     bouncing fireballs that set monsters on fire and burst when they hit
    Frost Wand      a fast freezing bolt: chills monsters, and sometimes freezes them solid
    Void Orb        a slow sphere that drags monsters in, then EXPLODES after a few seconds
    Lightning Rod   calls lightning down onto the monster you aim at - and it jumps to two more
    Abyss Tome      a fan of five small shadow bolts that curve toward monsters
  All five can be crafted (see the recipe book) or found in Ancient Ruins chests and as monster drops.


DANGEROUS NIGHTS
----------------
  After dark there are more monsters, rarer ones (the NIGHT STALKER - tall, fast, and it BLINKS behind you),
  night-only creatures (Moon Moths that flutter around lamps, Gloom Owls) and plants that only exist at
  night: MOONBLOOMS open on grass after dark and wilt at dawn (pick them for Moonpetals). Villagers whisper
  warnings and wait at their doors. Ancient braziers light themselves.


THE BLOOD MOON
--------------
  Every few nights (never the first three) the night is a BLOOD MOON: the sky bleeds, the moon is huge and
  red, the music turns into a dark drone with a heartbeat, swarms of BLOOD BATS and CRIMSON HUSKS pour out
  of the dark, villagers panic and refuse to talk, and after half a minute the CRIMSON REAPER comes for you:
  a floating reaper with a scythe that blinks after you and calls bats. Survive until dawn.
  Rewards: Blood Shards (needed for the Abyss Tome), diamonds, rubies, golden apples, Mana Potions, and
  sometimes magic weapons. Test it with /bloodmoon (and /bloodmoon off).


ANCIENT RUINS  (/locate ruins, /tp ruins)
-----------------------------------------
  Buried temples, one every few hundred blocks, deep underground. Look for TWO STUB PILLARS with a hatch of
  cracked bricks between them: dig it open and climb down the LADDER SHAFT.
    * a trap corridor: Ruin Spikes (jump them!) and DART TRAPS that fire when you step in their line
    * a secret room under the corridor: break the 3 cracked bricks between the two glowing runic tiles
      (bones rattle in there) and open the chest for the RUIN KEY
    * the statue hall: two of the statues wake up as SENTINELS; a treasure chest
    * a ladder down to the antechamber and the ANCIENT DOOR: right-click it holding the Ruin Key
    * the arena of THE HOLLOW KNIGHT, and behind a cracked wall, his hoard
  THE HOLLOW KNIGHT (mini-boss, 430 health)
    PHASE 1  slow, with a SHIELD. It blocks everything from the front (hit it from behind or while it
             swings - the wind-up shows a red arc), does 3-cut sword combos and shield bashes.
    PHASE 2  at half health the shield SHATTERS. Much faster, hits harder, leaps and slams the floor
             (two shockwaves), and calls skeletons up out of the ground.
    Drops: the HOLLOW BLADE (every third cut throws a wave of soul), 6-9 Ancient Armor Plates and Ancient
    Metal (Ancient Armor: 12 armor, +2 damage, +2 hearts, hurts whoever hits you), and sometimes the
    HOLLOW SIGNET (a charm: less damage from everything).
  /summon hollowknight  fights it anywhere.


OTHER THINGS
------------
  * Tiles are 16-bit now, so there is room for hundreds of new blocks.
  * 16 new advancements (and 9 new accessories to wear: see Skins > Accessories).
  * New commands: /bloodmoon, /locate ruins|redwood|frost|ash, /tp ruins|redwood|frost|ash,
    /summon <any new monster>.

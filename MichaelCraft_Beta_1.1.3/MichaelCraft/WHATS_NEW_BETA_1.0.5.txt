MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.0.5 - THE MECHANICS UPDATE
==============================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.0.5, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.0.5.
Older worlds load fine (your charms become level I).


SMOOTHER MINING
---------------
  - A cursor that wobbles a little over a block's edge no longer makes you switch
    blocks and lose your progress.
  - Half-dug blocks keep their cracks for 4 seconds (like Terraria). Switch to
    another block and come back, and you carry on where you left off.
  - The cracks sit on the block you're actually digging, and the block darkens
    smoothly as it gives.
  - Dig taps keep an even rhythm (about 4 per block) instead of a fixed tick.

SMOOTHER BUILDING
-----------------
  - Hold right-click and drag: a block goes down the moment your cursor reaches a
    new tile (no waiting for the next "tick").
  - A fast drag fills every tile it passed over, so lines of blocks come out solid
    instead of gappy (up to 10 tiles at once, all within your reach).
  - A little puff of the block when you place it.


CHARM LEVELS (I, II, III)
-------------------------
  Put TWO charms of the same kind and level in any crafting grid: they fuse into
  ONE charm of the next level (so a level III takes four level I's).
  Villagers and recipes make level I. The level shows in the corner of the slot
  (white I, blue II, gold III) and in the tooltip.

                   I              II             III
    Spring        jump x1.25     x1.35          x1.45  (about twice as high)
    Swift         +25% speed     +35%           +45%
    Warrior       +25% melee     +35%           +50%   (at least +1 / +2 / +3)
    Heart         +5 hearts      +6             +8
    Cloud         double jump,   double jump,   TRIPLE jump,
                  dash 0.9 s     dash 0.65 s    dash 0.45 s
    Feather       fall 3.2/s     2.4/s          1.7/s
    Power         ~10 blocks     ~15 blocks     ~22 blocks of flight (faster too)

  New advancement: "Maxed Out" - fuse a charm to level III.


ANGEL WINGS (new charm)
-----------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   G P G      G = Block of Gold (4)
                             D F D      P = Power Charm
                             G . G      F = Feather Charm
                                        D = Diamond (2)
  - Power Flight AND Slow Falling in ONE charm slot, and you steer faster while
    gliding.
  - Its level is the LOWER of the two charms you made it from (a Power II + a
    Feather III make Wings II). Fuse two Wings for the next level, like any charm.
  - Real pixel-art wings on your back, drawn in the same pixel size as your
    character: folded on the ground, spread while you glide, beating while you fly.
    Friends see them in multiplayer.
  - Flying on them is ALL feathers, no Power Charm fire: every wing beat throws a
    flurry of pixel feathers and a puff of air under you with a soft whump (the
    Ender Dragon's wing sound, pitched up), gold-and-white sparkles trail off the
    wing tips, and while you glide the air streams off them and a feather drifts
    away now and then.
  - New advancement: "Earn Your Wings".
  - Icon: Textures/items/angel_wings.png (16x16 - replace it with your own art if
    you like).


PIXEL PARTICLES
---------------
  - Every particle is drawn on the same pixel grid as the blocks, in whole pixels:
    mining chips are real bits of the block's own texture (like Minecraft), sparks
    are pixel squares and plus signs, feathers and petals are pixel sprites, and
    particles fade in steps instead of smoothly.
  - Light (torch glow, fireballs, auras) stays soft and smooth, like before.


THE DESPERATION PHASE: REBUILT LIKE INFERNUM'S SUPREME CALAMITAS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Rebuilt from the video you sent (Infernum's Supreme Calamitas desperation), and
  a lot faster:
  - INFINITE FLIGHT for everyone from the moment it starts ("Infinite flight
    granted!"): hold Space to fly up, let go to drop, hold S to dive. You fly as
    fast as you sprint and turn sharply. No fall damage.
  - The arena is walled in by BRIMSTONE FIRE (left, right and the roof) with a dark
    seam on the edge. Touching it hurts and you can't leave once you're in.
  - He never sits still: he flies around you, picking a new spot every ~2 seconds,
    and touching him hurts.
  - PATTERNS, NOT RANDOM ATTACKS. Every pattern plays out the same way every time,
    with one clear way through it that you can learn (its name shows under the
    boss bar):
      CORRIDOR  thin red lines warn, then pillars erupt from the floor EVERYWHERE
                except a 5-block gap. The gap steps 3 blocks along each time and
                bounces off the walls. Answer: follow the gap.
      HEX       he stops in the middle; a white flash, pink rings bloom, red lines
                shoot out of him, then a dart flies down every line. Each hex is
                turned exactly HALF a gap from the last. Answer: sit between two
                lines and slide half a gap each time. (II, III: a second set right
                after, turned half a gap again)
      SNAKE     walls of darts fall from the roof; the gap in each wall follows a
                smooth snake from side to side. Answer: weave with the snake.
                (II, III: bombs on alternate sides, with a dotted ring showing where
                and how wide they'll burst)
      SPIRAL    he spins in the middle firing arms of darts in pulses. Answer: circle
                him WITH the spin - it reverses (he roars when it does) - or slip
                through an arm between two pulses.
  - Each round plays all four a little faster; a white flash between rounds clears
    the screen and gives you 3 hearts back.
      0:00 Corridor  0:12 Hex  0:24 Snake  0:36 Spiral  0:48 flash
      0:52 ...round II...  1:40 flash   1:44 ...round III...  2:32 flash
      2:36 FINALE (a slow spiral and a gentle snake), then he swells with light
      and falls in a white flash at 2:50, as the song ends
  - Easier than before: darts and pillars hit softer (2-3 a hit in Diamond armour),
    1.4 seconds of safety after a hit, and the 3 hearts back between rounds.
    A test bot that just follows each pattern's answer survived the whole fight
    easily; one that flew around without dodging didn't.
  - Projectile effects: darts have a white-hot head, a fading afterimage trail and
    pixel embers; orbs sparkle; bombs fizz with a dotted burst ring; pillars throw
    embers up their column; warning lines have a spark running down them.
  - It lasts the whole song (the song is 2:52; he falls as it ends). For exactly
    2:30, set DESP.length to 150 in index.html.
  - To tune it: DESP_DMG (damage), DESP.iframes (safety after a hit), and the
    [I, II, III] numbers in each pattern in kingDesperation.


THE KING ATTACKS EVERYONE
-------------------------
  - In multiplayer, EVERY player at the fight gets their own fan of fireballs, the
    meteor storm falls around every player, and each claw goes after a different
    player (the next round picks the next players).
  - The Desperation phase already aims at everyone (rain, pillars, volleys,
    Gigablasts, and a gap in the Final Nova for each player).


THE HUD: BIGGER, AND MINECRAFT-STYLE
------------------------------------
  - A GUI scale like Minecraft's "Auto": the HUD is drawn in GUI pixels and blown
    up by the biggest whole number that fits your screen (3x on 1280x720, 4x on
    1920x1080), so it's much bigger and every pixel stays square and sharp.
    Options > HUD Size: Small / Normal / Large moves it one step down or up.
  - The hotbar is one pixel-art picture now (182x22, stone-grey frame, see-through
    cells) with a chunky white frame on the selected slot.
  - Hearts, hunger, armour and air: your Textures/HUD art, spaced like Minecraft's.
  - Your charms show at the top-left in light-grey bevelled boxes (like Minecraft's
    potion-effect boxes) with their level.
  - The buttons in the corner are stone buttons (they turn blue when you hover).
  - The boss bar is a thin, notched pixel bar with the name over it and the health
    under it (purple during the Desperation).
  - Messages and chat sit bottom-left on dark strips, like Minecraft's chat.

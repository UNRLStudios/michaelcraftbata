MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.0.3 - THE KING FIGHTS BACK
===============================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.0.3, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.0.3.
Worlds from 1.0.1 and 1.0.2 load fine.

The King of the Abyss is now meant to be fought from range: bows and muskets.


KING OF THE ABYSS
-----------------
  - 825 health (was 520). "Bosses Scale With Players" still adds +50% for each extra
    player near him when he spawns.
  - MELEE ARMOUR: close-up hits only do HALF damage (swords, axes, fists, katanas,
    including katana dash cuts). The Hammer is the exception: it still hits for full.
    Arrows and musket shots also do full damage.
    You'll see a purple flash when he soaks up a hit, and the first time it
    happens you get a hint to use a bow or musket.
  - BLEEDING HEALS HIM: when the Sensei's Katana makes him bleed, the bleed heals
    him 40-50 health over its 3 seconds instead of hurting him (green "+" numbers).
    Ruby Arrows still make him bleed normally, so that bleed still hurts him.
  - Friends can't shove him around with knockback any more.
  - To tune him, see the BOSSES entry for 'abyssking' in index.html:
      meleeTaken: 0.5       (how much melee damage gets through)
      bleedHeal: [40, 50]   (how much a katana bleed heals him)
    Any boss added later can use the same two settings.


ARROWS: +25% DAMAGE
-------------------
  Arrow ................ 4  -> 5
  Iron-Tipped Arrow .... 6  -> 7.5
  Diamond-Tipped Arrow . 8  -> 10
  Ruby Arrow ........... 10 -> 12.5


FIXED: DOING THINGS THROUGH WALLS
---------------------------------
  This bug was in the whole game: you could mine, open, place blocks against and
  hit anything within reach, even if it was behind solid blocks.
  - Now a line is traced from your eyes to your cursor, block by block. The first
    solid block in the way is what you mine or use, like in Minecraft.
  - You can't open a chest, furnace or anything else through a wall.
  - You can't target or hit monsters (or players, with Friendly Fire on) you
    can't see. The sword sweep, katana slashes and the Hammer's shockwave only hit
    what they can actually see.
  - Windows work: if you can see part of it (head, middle or feet), you can hit it.
  - Glass, leaves and other solid blocks count as walls. Torches, ladders,
    platforms, water and other non-solid blocks don't block you.


SMALL FIXES
-----------
  - A friend's Ruby Arrow bleed and katana dash bleed now actually reach the host
    (before, only the friend's screen showed the bleed).


BOSS FIXES (found after the first 1.0.3 build)
----------------------------------------------
  - FIXED: the King vanished about 2 seconds after spawning if you were in
    CREATIVE mode (single player or multiplayer). This came from 1.0.2: creative
    players weren't counted as being at the fight, so he thought everyone had left.
    Creative players count again. He still can't hurt you in creative, like before.
  - FIXED (an older bug): if you got more than 120 blocks away from the King, the
    normal "clear out far-away monsters" cleanup deleted him without telling the
    game. Then the altar said "The King is already awake!" and you couldn't summon
    him again. Now bosses are never cleaned up like normal monsters. Run far
    enough away and he leaves properly (after 4 seconds), and you can summon him again.

  Tested: altar and /summon, survival and creative, single player; host/friend in
  every creative/survival combo in multiplayer; getting hit, dying, running away,
  and killing him (loot + advancement) in both.

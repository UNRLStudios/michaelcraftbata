MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.0.4 - THE ABYSS UPDATE
===========================================

NOTE: the version is now beta-1.0.4, so everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.0.4.
Older worlds load fine.


ABYSSAL ARMOR (new)
-------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   B A B      B = Block of Ruby (7)
                             B H B      A = Samurai Armor
                             B B B      H = Abyssal Heart
  - 10 defense
  - +5 damage to EVERYTHING you do (swords, katanas, hammers, bow, musket)
  - +3 extra hearts
  - Crimson-and-void look when you wear it (it uses the Samurai body shape).


ABYSSAL WAR HAMMER (new)
------------------------
  Recipe (Crafting Table):   B B B      B = Block of Ruby (5)
                             B H B      H = Abyssal Heart
                             . S .      S = Sledgehammer (where the stick usually goes)
  - Works like the Sledgehammer, but 14 base damage per smash (the pound does 17).
  - RAGE: every 10th swing it glows purple and for 2 seconds you can swing every
    0.1 seconds.
  - Hammers aren't slowed by the King's melee armour, and it takes Sharpness
    at the Enchanting Table.


THE ABYSSAL HEART
-----------------
  No longer just a trophy: it's what makes Abyssal Armor and the Abyssal War Hammer.
  The King drops one every time you beat him.


BALANCE
-------
  - Katanas (Katana and Sensei's Katana): one attack every 0.5 seconds
    (was 0.22 / 0.19). The dash is unchanged.
  - Tonio's Musket: 45 damage per shot (was 22), and reloads in 1.7 s (was 2.1).


KING OF THE ABYSS: DESPERATION PHASE (rebuilt from scratch)
------------------------------------------------------------
  Built like a Terraria desperation phase, but tuned for the FIRST boss.

  At 1% health he refuses to die. Nothing can hurt him now (no single hit can
  skip it), everything he'd thrown vanishes and his imps are sacrificed.

  GET READY (10 seconds): a big countdown, Final Stand fades out, and glowing
  walls appear around the arena. Get between them (once you're in, you can't
  leave until it's over).

  Then his own song starts (Sounds/music/desperation_BGM.mp3) and the boss bar
  becomes his LAST STRENGTH, draining over 53 seconds. When it's empty he dies on
  his own and drops his loot. You win by SURVIVING.

     0-10 s   CRIMSON RAIN       Orbs fall through the roof. Every 1.5 s one lands
                                 right above you: keep moving.
    10-21 s   BRIMSTONE PILLARS  Warned columns of fire. Some chase you (4 in a
                                 row), some FENCE the whole floor: stand in a gap.
                                 The last fence comes twice, shifted half a gap.
    21-31 s   HELLFIRE           Aimed fans of 3 orbs, plus Gigablasts that burst
                                 into a ring over your head.
    31-42 s   SHOCKWAVES         His claws go up and the floor line flashes
                                 "JUMP!" (it turns white just before the slam). Two
                                 fire waves roll out from under him along the floor
                                 (and over the dais): jump as one reaches you.
    42-47 s   LAST BREATH        Alternating rings of orbs (each fills the gaps of
                                 the last) and a little rain.
    47-53 s   FINAL NOVA         He gathers his strength, then fires one huge ring.
                                 The white cone and the "SAFE" mark show the one gap
                                 (one per player, a couple of blocks from you).

  What keeps it fair (and skill-based):
    - every attack is warned first, with a short breather between acts
    - one idea at a time; orbs fly 6-10 blocks a second (you sprint at 8.6)
    - the only thing you MUST jump is a shockwave, timed for your jump
    - a hit gives you 1 second of safety and only a small push (no chain hits)
    - standing still gets you hit; moving keeps you safe

  DAMAGE (after the King's x1.6; armour takes a flat amount off):
    orb 11, wave 11, pillar 13, Gigablast 13, nova 14, the walls 10.
    In Diamond armour: 4, 4, 6, 6, 7, 3 - about 4-5 mistakes before you die.

  Playtests (Diamond armour, Normal, the whole 63 seconds):
    - a bot that just reads the warnings took 4 hits (16 of 20 health) and won;
    - standing still took 16 hits (76 damage) in 30 seconds - dead by about 11 s;
    - shockwaves: jumping when the wave is about 1.5-3 blocks away clears it
      every time (a window of about a quarter of a second).

  New sounds (from the Minecraft pack): a totem when he refuses to die, a conduit
  when it starts, an earth-crack for the slams, thunder for the Final Nova and a
  respawn-anchor drain when his strength runs out.

  Testing: with cheats on, /summon king, then /desperation skips straight to it.

  To tune it (index.html):
    BOSSES > abyssking > desperation: { at: 0.01, music: ... }
    DESP = { grace: 10, length: 53, iframes: 1.0, kb: [4, 3], wall: 18.5 }
    DESP_DMG = { orb: 7, wave: 7, pillar: 8, blast: 8, nova: 9, border: 6 } (before x1.6)
    DESP_ACTS (when each act starts and what he says), and the numbers inside each
    act in kingDesperation.


BEDS SET YOUR SPAWN POINT
-------------------------
  - Right-click a bed at ANY time of day: "Respawn point set". (Sleeping at night
    still skips to morning and heals you.)
  - You respawn next to your bed. If the bed is broken or blocked, you're told so
    and go back to world spawn.
  - A bed inside a building respawns you at that building's front door.
  - In multiplayer, every player has their own bed. It's saved with the world.

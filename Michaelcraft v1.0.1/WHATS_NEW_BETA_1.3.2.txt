MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.3.2 - THE MOON
==================================

(Everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.3.2: the version is now beta-1.3.2. Worlds you already have are fine.)


THE ROCKET GOES TO THE MOON  (Space Detention add-on)
-----------------------------------------------------
  Right-click the Cockpit of your finished rocket and it doesn't just fly away any more: it flies to THE MOON.
    * Countdown, liftoff, up through the sky into black space...
    * ...then the CRUISE: Earth shrinks away below you while the Moon swells up to fill the sky...
    * ...a flash, and the rocket comes down on its engines onto the Moon's LANDING PAD (dust everywhere).
  The first time you land, Space Detention is beaten: "YOU ESCAPED!" (you still get the 2 Mana Stars and
  8 Diamonds). Then: Explore the Moon, or Save and Quit.
  The rocket waits on the pad. Right-click its Cockpit to FLY HOME (Earth grows, you land back on your own
  launch site), and again to go back up. As many trips as you like.
  * Worlds that already escaped in 1.3/1.3.1: the rocket is gone, but your launch site's blueprint is back.
    Build a new rocket on it and fly to the Moon.
  * Multiplayer: the host launches and EVERYONE flies along. A friend who joins while the host is up there
    starts on the Moon too.
  * Cheats: /moon and /earth take you there and back straight away (no rocket needed).


THE MOON
--------
  Its own world, with its own vibe:
    * A black sky with a purple-and-blue nebula, three layers of stars, shooting stars, a fierce little
      white sun, and the EARTH hanging up there, slowly turning (clouds, ice caps, and city lights on its
      night side). Far-off crater ridges stand on the horizon.
    * No day and no night: a hard, silver light, always.
    * LOW GRAVITY: huge floaty jumps, slow falls, falls hurt far less, and moon dust puffs up when you land
      or run.
    * Its own MUSIC, played live: a slow, spacey pad, a deep drone and bells echoing in the dark.
    * The land: grey Moon Dust over Moon Rock, wide flat dark "seas" of Lunar Basalt, rough highlands, and
      CRATERS everywhere (big ones with raised rims and a central peak, lots of little ones).
    * Underground: long winding LAVA TUBES, and deep hollow GEODES lined with glowing Moon Crystals.
    * The landing pad has a lamp post on each side (you can't break the pad, or the rocket while it's up there).
    * If you die on the Moon you wake up next to the rocket (or in a bed you put on the Moon).

  NEW BLOCKS:  Moon Dust, Moon Rock, Lunar Basalt, Lunar Ore, Moon Crystals (glow), Meteorite (glows; a
               fallen star sits in the middle of most big craters), Moon Cheese (yes, really: a rare pocket
               deep down), Moon Bricks (4 Moon Rock), Lunar Lamp (the brightest light there is).
  NEW ITEMS:   Lunarite (from Lunar Ore), Moon Crystal, Star Fragment (from Meteorites), Moon Cheese (a big meal
               that heals you for a while), Star Jelly (restores mana and makes you swift).
  LUNAR TOOLS: Lunar Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel and Sword (3 / 3 / 1 / 2 Lunarite + sticks). Faster than Ember,
               they mine anything, and the Lunar Sword's hits throw monsters UP into the air.
  RECIPES:     Lunar Lamp x4  = 4 Glass + 4 Moon Crystals + 1 Star Fragment (G C G / C S C / G C G)
               Mana Star      = 3 Star Fragments + 1 Moon Crystal

  MOON MONSTERS (they only live up there; lamps and torches keep them away from your base):
    Crater Crawler   a rocky bug with glowing violet cracks. Big floaty hops at you, and a bite.
    Star Jelly       a glowing jellyfish that drifts above the dust. Harmless. Drops Star Jelly.
    Astral Wisp      keeps its distance and throws curving bolts of starlight.
    Selenite Golem   rare and huge. Winds up and slams the ground.

  NEW ADVANCEMENTS (each with an accessory):  One Small Step (land on the Moon) - Earthrise (fly home) -
    It IS Made of Cheese! - Moonlighting (Lunar Pickaxe) - Catch a Falling Star (a Star Fragment)


NEW FONT: MINECRAFTIA
---------------------
  All the writing in the game (menus, the HUD, signs, damage numbers...) is in MINECRAFTIA now, like
  Minecraft's own lettering. It's built into the game, so it works everywhere with nothing to install.
  (Minecraftia normally sits too high in its boxes: the built-in copy is fixed so text is centred.)


CREATE NEW WORLD fits on one screen
-----------------------------------
  The Create New World menu is two columns now (name and username side by side, game mode and cheats side
  by side, game rules and add-ons side by side, Create and Back at the bottom): no more scrolling.

MichaelCraft v1.0.1

HARDMODE MONSTER DAMAGE, CROSSHAIR ON THE MENU
- Hardmode monsters now hit 9x as hard as normal (was 1.5x). Armor up.
- The custom crosshair (and its click animation) is now your pointer on the main menu and every other screen too.

HARDMODE MONSTERS, CROSSHAIR
- Hardmode monsters now have 8x their normal health (was 1.6x). Bring your best sword.
- The custom crosshair works everywhere in a world now, including the inventory, chests and menus (it used
  to turn back into the normal mouse there).
- The crosshair animates when you click: it squeezes in and lights up while you hold the button, then pops
  back out. Its tip stays exactly on the mouse the whole time.

BOSS HEALTH NUMBER FIX
- Fixed: after you beat a boss (or lost to one), its health number stayed stuck at the top of the screen.
  The boss bar now slides fully off the screen and its number goes with it.

CUTSCENE BARS, CRYSTAL HEART
- Fixed: the black cutscene bars (the Slayer's and the King of the Abyss') were drawn UNDER the hotbar, hearts
  and boss bar. They now go over the HUD (dialogs and menus still show on top).
- The Crystal Heart no longer drops the first time you beat the Slayer of Worlds before Hardmode. You get it
  from her Hardmode rematch, like before.

SLAYER'S SLASH FLURRY NERFED, SINGULARITY SWORD REACH
- Slayer of Worlds, Infinite Slashes / Frenzy Slashes (the flurry near the end of the fight): 6 cuts (was
  10 / 8), with more time between them, a longer warning before each one, narrower cuts and less damage.
  Getting hit by one now protects you for 1.1 s, so one mistake can't chain into the whole flurry.
- Singularity Sword: its Event Horizon crescents fly almost twice as far (40 blocks, was 22) and faster.

PRISMHEART BUFF
- 36 damage (was 32), 20% critical hits (was 18%), and its ghosts can hit the same enemy again a little sooner.

SLAYER OF WORLDS (THE SWORD): REFORGED
- A brand new animated texture: the red core pulses up the blade, light runs round the guard ring, and
  sparks of void drift off it. It's drawn bigger in your hand too.
- Stronger than Hellfire now: 36 damage (was 30), a cut every 0.21 s (was 0.26 s), longer reach.
- Crescents hit harder, fly faster and pierce more enemies. The third cut throws FIVE (was three).
- The dash recharges in 0.5 s (was 0.85 s), goes further, and hits for 60 (was 38).
- NEW, WORLD REND: the first cut after a dash throws a giant crescent that goes through everything.

SINGULARITY SWORD: MORE DAMAGE
- Damage 58 (was 48), and a cut every 0.17 s (was 0.18 s).
- Event Horizon crescents, Micro-Singularities and Orbital Fragments all hit harder.

SINGULARITY SWORD BUFF
- Riftwalk (right-click) recharges in 1.8 s (was 5 s) and reaches 20 blocks (was 18). Its cut hits harder.
- Damage 48 (was 40), and it swings a little faster. 25% critical hits (was 22%).
- Event Horizon crescents hit harder, fly faster and further.
- A Micro-Singularity every 3rd swing (was every 4th), and it grinds harder.
- 4 Orbital Fragments (was 3), firing faster and harder.

THE SINGULARITY IS MUCH HARDER
- 85,000 health (was 60,000), and it hits harder (touching it, and its dash, hurt more too).
- It reads your movement: dashes, lasers and bolt fans aim at where you're GOING, every other shot. Running
  in a straight line won't save you anymore.
- More of everything: 4 dashes (5 in the second form) that shed bolts as they go, 3-4 lasers that sweep,
  more and faster orbs, more shards, a faster ring with smaller gaps, a stronger pull, bigger bolt fans.
- Second form: it STACKS attacks. Shard rain or a void often lands on top of whatever it's doing.
- NEW THIRD PHASE, EVENT HORIZON (at a quarter health): space itself drags you toward it for the rest of
  the fight, it gets even faster, fires lasers out of both sides, and opens more voids at once.

THE SINGULARITY IS MUCH FASTER NOW
- It never sits still: between attacks it circles you fast, swaps sides and fires fans of void bolts.
- NEW ATTACK, VOID DASH: a lane of light shows where it's going, then it rips straight through it, 3 times in a row
  (4 in its second form, and each dash leaves a burst of bolts). Step out of the lane.
- Almost no downtime between attacks. Lasers charge faster and fire 2 shots (3 in the second form). Orbs launch
  sooner, shards fall faster, the ring and the pull are quicker, and it keeps moving and shooting during them.
- Teleport is snappier, and it comes out of it already attacking. The intro and phase change are shorter, and it
  opens its second form with a dash.
- Fixed: it could use the same attack twice in a row.

FIX: WHITE SCREEN DURING THE SINGULARITY FIGHT
- FIXED: the screen could turn solid white (and go glitchy when zooming) during the Singularity fight.
  When the Singularity teleported, its glow was drawn at a tiny negative size for a split second, which crashed
  that frame's lighting pass and left the screen stuck in "add light" mode, so every frame piled up to white.
- The teleport no longer does that, every effect radius is clamped, and the lighting/overlay passes now always
  clean up after themselves even if one effect breaks, so a single bad effect can't white out the screen again.

NEW BOSS: THE SINGULARITY (the first Hardmode boss, harder than the Slayer)
  A collapsed star wrapped in void-crystal: a black hole for an eye, halos and rings of torn matter
  turning around it, crystals hanging in the air like knives. 60,000 health.
  How to call it: in Hardmode, make a COLLAPSED STAR at the Magical Workbench
      Voidsteel Ingot, Shattered Crystal, Voidsteel Ingot
      Shattered Crystal, Void Crystal, Shattered Crystal
      Voidsteel Ingot, Shattered Crystal, Voidsteel Ingot
    and use it anywhere on the surface. (/summon singularity works too.)
  Its attacks (every one is warned first, and every one has a way out):
    VOID LASER          a thin line marks where it will fire, then a massive beam. Second form: it sweeps.
    GRAVITATIONAL PULL  a well that drags you in for a few seconds, then explodes: be outside its ring.
    ORBITAL FRAGMENTS   void orbs circle it, then launch and home in on you (they turn slowly: sidestep).
    REALITY SHARDS      columns of light mark the ground, then crystal shards fall from the sky.
    SINGULARITY RING    a ring of energy closes in around you and opens back out: slip through the glowing gap.
    TELEPORT            it folds itself away and unfolds somewhere else.
    FINALE (second form) five small voids at once: each one pulls, spits rings of bolts, then collapses.
  At half health it COLLAPSES into its second form: faster, more of everything, voids circling it.
  When it dies it falls into itself and is gone in a flash of light.
  It drops SINGULARITY FRAGMENTS (3 the first time), Voidsteel Ingots, Void Crystals, Shattered Crystals,
  Mana Stars, Diamonds and Emeralds.
  Its code is in Mobs/Bosses/singularity.js.

NEW SWORD: THE SINGULARITY SWORD (the best sword in the game)
  At the Magical Workbench: a Singularity Fragment above the Prismheart. 40 damage, a cut every 0.2 s,
  22% critical hits. Its picture is animated: void energy pulses up the blade and the rims of its two black
  holes turn.
    - Every swing cuts AND throws an EVENT HORIZON: a crescent of void that flies at your cursor, through

    - Every 4th swing opens a MICRO-SINGULARITY at your cursor: a little black hole that drags enemies in,

      leave cuts everything along the way, and you can't be hurt mid-step.
    - Three ORBITAL FRAGMENTS circle you while you hold it and fire homing void bolts at anything near.
  Its code is in Items/singularity.js.

NEW SWORD: HELLFIRE (the strongest sword before Hardmode)
  27 damage, a cut every 0.3 s, long reach, and it sets everything it touches on fire.
  How to make it (all at a Crafting Table):
    HELLFIRE BASE:   Demon Horn, Hellstone Ingot, Demon Horn / Hellstone Ingot, Hellfire Cleaver, Hellstone Ingot /
                     Demon Horn, Hellstone Ingot, Demon Horn
    HELLFIRE BLADE:  Hellstone Ingot, Hellforged Sword, Hellstone Ingot / Hellstone Ingot, Hollow Blade, Hellstone Ingot /
                     Hellstone Ingot, Ember Sword, Hellstone Ingot
    HELLFIRE:        the Base and the Blade together
  What it does:

NEW HARDMODE CHARM: THE COMET CHARM
  Wear it and double-tap Space (or the jump button) to take off. Then you fly as long as you like:
  A/D left and right, W or Space up, S down, and hold Sprint for the afterburner (x1.5, with a sonic boom).
  It's FAST: 24 blocks a second at tier I, up to 50 at tier VIII (75 with the afterburner).
  The handling is touchy: full speed almost at once, sharp turns, and you slide for a moment when you let go.
  Double-tap again to drop out of the sky, or fly down onto the ground to land. Hitting a wall at speed
  shakes the screen (it doesn't hurt). It leaves a glowing comet trail.
  Make it at the Magical Workbench:  Shattered Crystal, Mana Star, Shattered Crystal
                                     Shattered Crystal, Charm Frame, Shattered Crystal
                                     Shattered Crystal, Power Charm, Shattered Crystal
  (It comes out at the same tier as the Power Charm you put in. It merges up like any charm.)
  Its code is in Items/comet_charm.js.

THE PRISMHEART (a new post-Hardmode sword)
  It doesn't swing like a sword. Every swing throws the ghost of one of the ten swords it was forged from
  (Diamond, Ruby, Frost Blade, Ember Sword, Sensei's Katana, Parasite Katana, Hollow Blade, Hellforged,
  Voidsteel and the Slayer of Worlds). Each ghost flies a fast, wild loop out to whatever is near your cursor,
  up to 20 blocks away, and back to you. The ghosts fly straight through blocks and through every enemy in
  their way. Hold the button to keep swinging. 14% critical hits. No mana, no ammo, never breaks.
  It can be Supercharged like any weapon. (There are no Terraria-style modifiers like "Legendary" in
  MichaelCraft; the Supercharger's rarities, up to MYTHIC, are the closest thing.)
  How to get it:
    1. In Hardmode, beat any boss again (Parasite, Hollow Knight, King, Overlord: an Echo or the usual way).
       It shatters into 1 to 6 SHATTERED CRYSTALS. (The Slayer of Worlds never drops them.)
    2. Beat the Slayer of Worlds again in Hardmode. She doesn't explode this time: she lowers her sword, gives
       the nearest of you her CRYSTAL HEART, and sinks back down into her altar.
    3. MAGICAL WORKBENCH: 8 Shattered Crystals around a Crafting Table (at a Crafting Table).
    4. PRISMHEART: at the Magical Workbench, 8 Shattered Crystals around the Crystal Heart.
  The Prismheart, the Shattered Crystal and the Crystal Heart are animated (in your slots, in your hand,
  on the ground). Their code is in Items/prismheart.js; the Slayer's scene is in Mobs/Bosses/slayer_of_worlds.js.

EVERY MOB HAS ITS OWN FILE NOW (the new Mobs folder, next to index.html)
  Every creature and every boss is in its own file: Mobs/Animals, Mobs/Monsters, Mobs/NPCs, Mobs/Bosses
  (king_of_the_abyss.js, apex_parasite.js, hollow_knight.js, overlord.js, slayer_of_worlds.js...).
  Each file has, top to bottom: its STATS (health, damage, speed, size, knockback...), its PATHFINDING,
  its AI, its VISUALS (how it's drawn, its particles and effects), its SOUNDS and its LOOT.
  Change a number in a mob's file, reload, and the game uses it. Mobs/README.txt explains it all
  (and how to add a new mob). The Mobs folder has to be next to index.html, like Textures and Sounds.

SMARTER MONSTERS: PATHFINDING
  Monsters used to walk straight at you. Now a monster that's after you plans a route over the blocks
  (A* pathfinding, from its real size, with closed doors counted as walls):
    - it leaps a one-block hole instead of falling in and getting stuck, and plans its way round lava
    - it jumps up the ledges on the way, drops down where it has to, and stops at a drop it can't take
    - bats, imps, demons, wisps and wraiths fly round walls: hide in a hut and they find the doorway
    - a swooping bat only dives when it can actually see you (no more diving into the roof forever)
    - your dogs and cats find their way to you, and farm animals to you when you hold their food
  Each mob's file says how it moves (walk, climb, fly) and how far it will jump, drop and look.
  /mobpaths shows the routes they're planning (green: a route to you, orange: as close as it can get,
  blue: flying round a wall).

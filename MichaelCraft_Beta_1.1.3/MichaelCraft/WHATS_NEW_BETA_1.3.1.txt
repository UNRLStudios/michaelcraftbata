MICHAELCRAFT BETA 1.3.1
=======================

(Everyone in a multiplayer game needs 1.3.1: the version is now beta-1.3.1.)

THE ROCKET IS NOW BACKGROUND BLOCKS
-----------------------------------
  Every rocket part (hull, fins, engines, fuel tanks, cockpit, nose cone) is a walk-through block now, like the
  Crafting Table: you can stand inside the rocket while you build it, walk through it, and place parts around
  yourself. The Launch Platform itself is still solid ground.

CURSOR MODE (like Terraria's) - so you can really right click
-------------------------------------------------------------
  For touch screens and trackpads. A crosshair is your pointer:
    * DRAG anywhere on the screen to move it (it stays where you leave it)
    * HOLD the red L button to mine / attack (left click)
    * HOLD the blue R button to place / use / open things, or to rapid-fire the Short Bow (right click)
    * use one thumb on the screen and another on a button at the same time
    * the crosshair turns red or blue while you hold L or R
  Turn it on with the cross button next to the jump button (touch), with C, or in Options > Controls > Cursor
  Mode. On a keyboard, Z is left click and X is right click while it is on (you can rebind all three keys).
  The old mine / place toggle button is replaced by it while it is on.

SEE YOUR FRIENDS' SHOTS (multiplayer)
-------------------------------------
  Arrows, musket balls, spells (Ember Staff, Frost Wand, Void Orb, Abyss Tome...), the Hollow Blade's soul
  wave, the Frost Blade's shards and Lightning Rod's lightning now show up on everybody's screen, not just the
  shooter's. They are for show - the shooter's own game does the hitting. The monsters' spells (Frost Wraith
  shards, Ember Wisp fireballs, the Hollow Knight's soul waves...) are now sent to friends too, so you can see
  them coming, and they DO hurt the friend they hit.
